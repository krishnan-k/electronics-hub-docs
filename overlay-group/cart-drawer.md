# Cart drawer

Displays the slide-out cart that opens when a customer adds a product.

## How to access

1. **Go to** Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes.
2. **Click** Customize on your active theme.
3. Open **Theme settings** → **Cart** to choose the cart type and drawer behavior. The drawer content is edited through the **Cart** page template.

### Section Settings

This section has no settings of its own. Use its blocks to control the content.
